Paper and Lamplight

The paper foundation you liked, with Lamplight's atmosphere kept, in blue and white. One design with two grounds: paper in daylight, deep blue when the lamp is the only thing on.

What changed from the amber version. Yellow on black is gone. The single light in the room is now the patient's own words in pale blue, which reads as calm rather than as a warning, and it means one accent family works on both grounds instead of two.

Why two grounds rather than a light theme with a dark option. The 2am argument was the strongest thing in Lamplight, and it does not need its own design to be true. This is the same layout, the same type, the same components, lit differently.

Daylight

Paper, with somewhere for the light to fall

The ground is still paper and Sage still speaks in serif, so it is recognisably the design that was approved. What Lamplight contributes is depth: a soft wash from the top of the screen instead of a flat fill, cards that lift on a real shadow rather than sitting behind a hairline, and the patient's bubble carrying a faint bloom so their own words are the warmest thing on the page. The sage green becomes ink blue, which is the one thing here that a returning user will notice immediately.

Lamp on

The same app, at two in the morning

Not a separate design — the same components, lit from below instead of above. The ground is a deep blue rather than black, so it never goes to a dead flat void, and the one light in the room is still the patient's own words, now in pale blue. Sage speaks in near-white serif. Everything else recedes.

Every pair checked against WCAG AA on the ground it is actually painted on. The bloom is a background wash and a soft shadow, deliberately not a coloured glow: a neon halo is the tell this product should be furthest from. Building this is a token swap in mobile/constants/theme.ts plus a second set for the night ground; the approved 2026-08-24 palette stays verbatim at mobile/constants/theme.v1.ts and at the tag design-v1-approved-mockups.
